# v1.0 Plan — Regional Ocean Modelling in the Arabian Gulf
**Subtitle:** *Wave and Circulation Hindcast Skill Assessment, v1.0*

This plan locks the scope, structure, evidence base, and execution sequence for v1.0 of the Gulf hindcast skill assessment report (Asset 1 of three planned publications). All decisions captured here were reached in a planning session on 2026-05-02.

---

## Purpose and audience

Hybrid technical brief, 20–30 pp, ~15–25 references. Aimed at technical buyers who, after a first meeting, ask *"how good is the model?"* and need substantive evidence — not a peer-reviewed paper, not a one-page skim (the one-pager is Asset 3).

The report is a "live document": versioned filename on the cover, public changelog at the back, regenerable via Quarto. v1.x updates are anticipated as multi-year coupled wave runs, currents validation, and the satellite-derived southern Gulf bathymetry become available.

## Scope discipline (rules adopted from earlier publication-discipline guidance)

- **Hindcast under reanalysis (ERA5) forcing only.** Operational forecast skill verification is a future v1.x item once enough operational archive exists. Title and methods make the distinction explicit.
- **"Skill assessment", not "validation"** — canonical metocean term per Mentaschi/Jolliff/Wilmott literature.
- **No "Backbone" branding in the title.** Reserved for marketing surfaces and earned only after a published nested-product case study.
- **No major model changes immediately before publication.** v1.0 reports the validated baseline. C05 AGRIF nesting test, satellite-derived southern Gulf bathymetry, and multi-year coupled WW3 runs are deliberately deferred to later versions where each becomes a v1.x headline upgrade.
- **Asymmetric maturity is fine and should be explicit.** Quantitative skill where defensible, model-derived characterisation where not, explicit "not yet validated" language for currents.

## Models reported

- **CROCO**: configuration `C04_I01_GLORYS_ERA5`. Coverage Jan 2015 → ~May 2025 (10+ years).
- **WW3**: configuration `RUN_02` (with CROCO surface currents and water levels via one-way coupling). Coverage Jan 2016 – Dec 2016 only. The 1-year evaluation window is an explicit limitation; multi-year extension is a v1.x deliverable.
- **Not reported in v1.0**: WW3 RUN_01 (no currents — superseded by RUN_02 methodologically even though improvement was modest in the specific 2016 satellite validation), CROCO C04_I02 (sibling hourly-output configuration for turbidity model input), CROCO C05_* (AGRIF nesting tests — saved for a separate future case study).

## Evidence base

| Variable | Evidence type | Source | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea surface temperature | OSTIA satellite L4 | Regional fields + point timeseries | Multi-year |
| Salinity | None usable (regional satellite SSS data quality untrustworthy in this region) | — | — |
| Salinity climatology | Model-only descriptive | CROCO monthly means + Aug/Feb sections | Multi-year |
| Water level | Tide gauge harmonic + non-tidal residual | Salmiya, Majis | Multi-year |
| Waves | CMEMS L3 satellite Hs (jason1/2/3, saral, cryo2, envi) | Co-located scatter + spatial maps | 2016 only |
| Currents | None | — | — (deferred to v1.1) |

**Excluded:** Fujeirah in-situ T/S observations (cannot be used). Mirfa-point CSVs in repo are CROCO extractions only — no observational counterparts.

## Table of contents (locked, flat structure)

1. **Introduction** — Gulf context, model purpose, scope (hindcast under reanalysis forcing, not operational forecast verification), live-document framing
2. **Model setup and methodology** — CROCO subsection (config, grid, bathymetry, forcing), WW3 subsection (config, grid, forcing including CROCO coupling), skill-assessment metric definitions, co-location procedure
3. **Temperature and salinity** — Monthly climatology of CROCO SST vs OSTIA, point timeseries vs OSTIA with stats, regional correlation/RMSE maps vs OSTIA *(new — to create)*, modelled salinity climatology (monthly maps + Aug/Feb sections, no validation)
4. **Water level** — Predicted (tidal) and non-tidal residual comparisons at Salmiya and Majis. The predicted comparison confirms modelled tides (and by association tidal currents) are decent; the non-tidal part is the operationally important component for ports.
5. **Waves** — RUN_02 vs satellite Hs over 2016, with explicit 1-year limitation note
6. **Limitations and v1.1 roadmap** — bathymetry upgrade, multi-year coupled wave run, currents quantitative validation, southern Gulf satellite-derived bathymetry
7. **Appendix** *(if time permits)* — namelist excerpts, full per-station tables
8. **Changelog** — v1.0 release notes; placeholder for v1.x entries

(Currents chapter deferred to v1.1; would otherwise have been Chapter 6.)

## New compute required for v1.0

Only one item: **SST regional correlation/RMSE maps vs OSTIA**. Adapt the WW3 RUN_02 `plot_spatial_stats.py` pattern using `crocotools_py/validation.py` for skill statistics. Co-located point timeseries vs OSTIA already exists in southern Gulf; needs extension to regional gridded maps.

Everything else in v1.0 is consolidation of existing artefacts in `oceanmotion-models/configs/gulf_01/`.

## Tooling and source

- **Format:** Quarto. Single source produces both PDF (primary deliverable) and HTML (optional, for website excerpts later). Native cross-references, citations, regeneration of figures from updated inputs.
- **Template:** sober report-style template (not the default academic look).
- **Repo:** new `oceanmotion-publications` (this repo). Asset 2 (turbidity climatology) and Asset 3 (capability one-pager) will live alongside under shared templates and bib.
- **Figures:** Quarto `freeze` mode. Code lives here, executes against model output paths in `~/code/oceanmotion-models/`, freezes results so subsequent builds don't depend on having model data present.

## Live-document mechanics

- Live-document framing stated in introduction with anticipated v1.x cadence.

## Bibliography intent

~15–25 references. Core citations:

- **CROCO**: Shchepetkin & McWilliams (2005), Auclair et al. (2022) for v1.3 features
- **WW3**: Tolman et al., WAVEWATCH III development team
- **Skill statistics**: Wilmott (1981), Jolliff et al. (2009), Mentaschi et al. (2013)
- **Gulf circulation**: Reynolds (1993), Pous et al., Yao (2008/2010), Akhter et al. (2021)
- **Forcing/data**: ERA5 (Hersbach et al.), CMEMS GLOBAL_REANALYSIS (Lellouche et al.), CMEMS L3 wave altimetry, OSTIA SST (Donlon et al.)
- **Tide-gauge harmonic analysis**: utide / Codiga (2011)
